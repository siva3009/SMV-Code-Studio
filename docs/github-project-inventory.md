## 3. Evaluation Criteria & Scoring Weighting

* **Technical Complexity (20 pts)**: Architecture depth, multi-tier systems, mathematical/AI difficulty.
* **Real-World Problem Solving (20 pts)**: Direct utility to businesses, students, or operational teams.
* **Engineering Quality (15 pts)**: Modularity, maintainability, type-safety, clean structure.
* **Feature Completeness (15 pts)**: End-to-end working capabilities from UI to persistence.
* **Originality & Innovation (10 pts)**: Proprietary algorithms, offline capability, AI vector integration.
* **Architecture & Scalability (10 pts)**: Microservices, separation of concerns, RBAC.
* **Documentation & Presentation (5 pts)**: Clarity of problem statement and instructions.
* **Relevance to TriCodeX Services (5 pts)**: Direct demonstration of agency capabilities.
